# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy and watch courses made by instructors.

## Language

### Purchases and refunds

**Purchase**:
A learner's paid access to one course.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction, enrollment

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to have a Purchase refunded, with their reason. It is Requested until a person on the team Approves or Declines it.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback, refund claim

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner when a Refund Request is Approved. It ends the Purchase, so the learner loses access to the course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, cancellation, chargeback

**Refund Window**:
The period after a Purchase during which the learner may raise a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: cancellation window, cooling-off period

**Chargeback**:
A payment dispute raised by the learner's bank or card issuer, not through Cadence. It is never another name for a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: using it for any request the learner makes in Cadence
